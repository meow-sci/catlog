catlog is a mod for Kitten Space Agency. It watches what happens while you fly,
writes it down, and periodically sends the record to a small server that folds it
into community leaderboards.

You never submit a score. There is no "post to leaderboard" button, and there is
nothing you can type. **Every number on every board is derived on the server from
what actually happened**, which is the whole point of the project — and the reason
this site exists at all. A leaderboard whose rules nobody can read is
indistinguishable from one somebody made up.

## What it records

Two different kinds of thing.

**Something happened.** A stage fired, two vehicles docked, you hit the ground, a
vehicle came apart, a kitten stepped outside. The mod is told about these directly
by the game, at the moment they happen.

**Sampled in the background.** Twice a second, the mod looks at every vehicle in
the world and notes altitude, speeds, acceleration and mass. Most of that is
folded into a 30-second summary rather than kept sample by sample. A few things —
crossing into an atmosphere, achieving orbit, arriving at a new world, touching
down on a surface, a kitten going over — are noticed by comparing one sample to
the last.

Every event carries which category it is in, and this site labels them
accordingly.

## What it does not record

Nothing about you personally, and nothing you type. The one thing it records that
did not come out of the flight is the handle you chose, and every kind of event
except six can be switched off individually in the mod's settings.

## How it behaves in your game

<Steps>

1. **It samples twice a second by default**, and folds that into one summary every
   30 seconds of game time. If the game hitches, missed samples are dropped rather
   than caught up on.

2. **It spools to a local database first.** Nothing is sent from the game thread.
   If you play offline, the record waits.

3. **It ships in compressed batches, never more often than every 30 seconds.**
   That floor is a hard constant enforced in three places.

4. **If the spool grows too large**, the background telemetry summaries are
   discarded first — they are the only kind of record catlog is willing to lose.
   Everything that scores is kept.

</Steps>

## What makes a flight not count

Some things a player can do would make a leaderboard meaningless. When catlog sees
one, it marks that flight, and a marked flight scores nothing at all — not the
record it was doing at the time, not the counters, nothing.

There are five: teleporting a vehicle, refilling its resources, draining them,
using the terminal's destroy command, and changing the kitten tumble tuning away
from stock.

Ordinary play never raises one. Walking a kitten out of an airlock teleports it
internally, and that path is deliberately excluded — flagging it would have
disqualified every EVA anybody ever did.
