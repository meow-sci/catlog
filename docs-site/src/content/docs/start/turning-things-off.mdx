catlog records 25 different kinds of thing. **You can switch almost any of them
off**, one at a time, in the mod's settings file — and the mod will keep doing
everything else exactly as before.

Nothing is switched off to begin with. The settings file carries the full list
written out as comments, with a note on each line saying what it feeds, so the
file itself tells you what you would be giving up before you give it up.

## What switching one off actually does

The mod stops producing that kind of record at all. Nothing is written down,
nothing is sent, and the server never learns there was anything to know.

That has one consequence and it is worth being blunt about it:

<Aside type="caution" title="Every switch costs data only that event can provide">
  There is no other source for it. Every number catlog publishes is worked out from what the mod
  reported, so a board fed only by a kind of event you turned off simply has nothing of yours to
  rank. Switching off the system body catalogue instead gives up catalogue, everywhere and future
  3D-system features. Your existing rows stay where they are — you just stop adding to them.
</Aside>

The reverse is also true, and it is the reason this page exists: **if a board of
yours looks empty when you expected a number on it, one of the possible causes is
that you turned its events off** — possibly a long time ago. The mod's status
window has a row that tells you. It reads _all reported_ when nothing is off, and
otherwise lists exactly what is.

## What each one costs you

| If you switch off            | You stop appearing on                                                                                                                    |
| ---------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| The system body catalogue    | no board directly; your save has no complete catalogue for everywhere badges or system views                                             |
| Situation changes            | Softest Touchdown, Bodies Landed On, Splashdowns                                                                                         |
| Landing                      | Softest Landing, Landings — and your landings stop appearing in the live feed                                                            |
| Atmosphere crossings         | Fastest Atmospheric Entry                                                                                                                |
| Orbit changes                | Orbits Achieved, Fastest to Orbit, Highest Apoapsis, Lowest Stable Orbit, Roundest Orbit, Most Inclined Orbit, Heaviest Payload To Orbit |
| Arriving at a new world      | Bodies Visited, and every Fastest to _somewhere_ board                                                                                   |
| Vehicles coming apart        | Rapid Unscheduled Disassemblies, and the board for each cause                                                                            |
| Impacts                      | Biggest Lithobrake Survived, Biggest Bang Survived                                                                                       |
| Staging                      | Stagings, Most Stages                                                                                                                    |
| Docking                      | Dockings                                                                                                                                 |
| Undocking                    | nothing today — it is recorded as part of the story, and no board reads it                                                               |
| Engine ignition              | Engines Lit                                                                                                                              |
| Engine shutdown              | nothing today                                                                                                                            |
| Running a tank dry           | Ran Dry                                                                                                                                  |
| Starting a spacewalk         | Spacewalks                                                                                                                               |
| Ending a spacewalk           | Longest Spacewalk                                                                                                                        |
| Kittens tumbling             | Kitten Tumbles                                                                                                                           |
| The roster snapshot          | Distance Travelled, Furthest-Travelled Kitten, Most Missions Flown — and your kittens stop appearing anywhere                            |
| The 30-second flight summary | Peak G Survived, Max Q Survived, Highest Altitude, Lowest Pass, Fastest Surface Speed, Fastest Orbital Speed                             |

Three of those deserve a sentence more.

**Landing** is the one most people will notice, because a landing is the most
ordinary thing on this list. Switching it off stops **Softest Landing** and
**Landings**, and stops your touchdowns showing up in the live feed. It does _not_
stop **Bodies Landed On** or **Softest Touchdown** — those come from the situation
change instead, and they answer different questions anyway. Nothing else records
how gently you came down.

**The roster snapshot** is where every per-kitten number comes from. Turning it
off does not just cost you three boards; your kittens stop being a thing catlog
knows about.

**The 30-second flight summary** is by far the largest amount of data catlog
sends, so it is the most tempting one to turn off. It is also the only kind of
record catlog is ever willing to discard on its own when the local spool fills up
— so turning it off leaves catlog nothing it can shed if you play offline for a
very long stretch. There is no substitute for it: the speeds on those boards are
not read from anywhere else, deliberately.

## The six you cannot switch off

Six kinds of record are refused. Set one of them to off and the mod ignores it,
says so in the log, and tidies the setting back out of the file the next time it
writes it.

They are not refused to make you send more. They are refused because switching
them off would not merely change what you _report_ — it would change what your
runs are _worth_ or detach them from the system that gives their world names
meaning.

| Always reported        | Why                                                                                                                                                                                                                                      |
| ---------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Identifying the system | This is what binds a save to the exact celestial catalogue its world names belong to. Without it “Luna” could mean different content and save- or system-scoped comparisons would have no honest home.                                   |
| A flight being marked  | This is the only thing that says a run was assisted. Without it, teleporting, refuelling, draining resources and using the terminal's destroy command would all score normally and appear publicly. It is the one that actually matters. |
| Losing a kitten        | This is what stops a crash that killed the crew being counted as a landing you walked away from.                                                                                                                                         |
| Starting a session     | The only record that a save was loaded. The time-to-milestone boards note it beside your row — they do not exclude you — but a "seconds from the start" figure means nothing if nobody can tell the clock went backwards.                |
| A flight beginning     | This is what makes a flight a flight. Without it there is no crew, no world and nothing for a board to attach to, so every flight you flew would be a fragment the server could not join up.                                             |
| A flight ending        | This is what makes something _recovered_. Without it nothing you land is ever counted as landed, and Kittens Recovered, Most Kittens Home At Once, Peak G Survived and Max Q Survived all score nothing.                                 |

Note what is **not** on that list: vehicles coming apart. How often you explode is
yours to keep to yourself. Whether you survived an impact is worked out on your
own machine before anything is written down, so that answer stays honest whether
or not you report the explosion.

<Aside type="note" title="This is a refusal to accept a setting, nothing more">
  Nothing about you is being judged, recorded or reported when the mod ignores one of those six.
  There is no note in your file, nothing sent to the server, and nothing remembered next time you
  play. The setting simply is not applied, and the log says which one and why.
</Aside>

## Being honest about what this is

Anyone willing to modify the mod itself can send whatever they like, and no amount
of this changes that. The six above exist so that switching off the thing that
keeps a leaderboard meaningful is not a one-line edit in a text file — which is a
much smaller claim, and the true one.
