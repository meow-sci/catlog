Three events, all sampled twice a second, all read at **whole-vehicle** level
rather than per engine.

<Aside type="caution" title="Whole-vehicle means what it says">
catlog asks the game two questions about the vehicle: is *any* engine running, and
is *any* propellant available. It does not track engines individually. Shutting
down one of two engine groups records nothing at all — the event fires when the
last one stops.

The per-engine reading exists in the game but is not exposed in a form the mod can
read without reaching into internals it has no business in, so the whole-vehicle
answer is the honest one.

</Aside>

## Flameout is reconstructed

Kitten Space Agency has no concept called "flameout". What it has is, per engine,
whether that engine is active and whether propellant is available to it.

catlog rebuilds the idea from the two whole-vehicle answers: engines running,
propellant gone, and no start or stop this tick. That is a genuine
approximation — a vehicle with one tank dry and another still feeding will not
report a flameout — and it is recorded here rather than hidden.

## None of these feed a board

All three are recorded and kept, and nothing reads them. They exist because burn
timing is the obvious next thing to build something out of, and an event log you
cannot go back and re-fold is worth much less than one you can.
