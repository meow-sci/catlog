Everything catlog records about the vehicle itself. Four of these are sampled
twice a second and reported when something changes; the rest fire the moment the
game does the thing.

## Where the altitudes and speeds come from

Three readings appear over and over, and it is worth being precise about them
once.

| Reading           | What it is                                                                                                                                                                                     |
| ----------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Altitude**      | Height above the body's **mean radius**, not above the terrain underneath you. Over a mountain your altitude is smaller than your height above sea level would suggest; over a trench, larger. |
| **Surface speed** | Speed relative to the surface rotating below you.                                                                                                                                              |
| **Orbital speed** | Speed relative to the body's centre.                                                                                                                                                           |

Surface speed is deliberately **not** whatever your navball is showing — the
navball changes meaning depending on which mode you have it in, and a leaderboard
cannot be built on a display setting.

The eight states are the game's own: `maneuvering`, `freefall`, `rolling`,
`landed`, `sailing`, `floating`, `dragging`, `bottomed`. They are two questions
combined — what you are touching (nothing, terrain, water, or both) and whether
you are on rails. A ninth value, `unknown`, appears if a future game build adds a
state catlog has not seen.

<Aside type="note" title="What counts as orbit">
  Your periapsis has to clear the top of the atmosphere by 1000 m. Around an airless body the bar is
  1000 m above the mean radius. Dropping back below it does not record anything — the bar simply
  re-arms, so a marginal orbit that decays and is raised again produces one achievement, not two.
</Aside>

Sphere-of-influence changes do double duty: they are how **Bodies Visited** counts,
and they are the arrival times behind every **Fastest to …** board. Both use the
name of the body directly. **There is no list of allowed worlds anywhere in
catlog** — a board exists because somebody went there.

## Impacts, and the frame that decides them

An impact is only interesting alongside whether the vehicle lived. That is harder
than it sounds, because the game applies every impact for a frame _before_ it
applies any destruction, and a destruction you asked for lands later still.

So catlog holds an impact for one full frame before deciding. A destruction of that
vehicle in the frame the impact happened, or the frame after, makes it
`survived: false`.

<Aside type="caution" title="Scuttling afterwards does not bank a record">
  Destroying the vehicle yourself right after a hard landing is treated exactly like the landing
  having destroyed it. Without that, every hard landing could be turned into a survived one.
</Aside>

Two more impacts that never count:

- **The launch pad.** Recorded, never scored.
- **Anything within 5 seconds of a teleport.** Discarded outright — the game
  suppresses the impact effects there, and it is not a landing.
