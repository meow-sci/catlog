Thirty-three fixed badges and all three place-name families can be awarded now. **Every World** and
**Nothing Left** are already part of the fixed catalogue, but their system-wide comparison is not
active yet.

<Aside type="note" title="A tier does not replace anything">
  The exploration and survival tiers are separate badges. Reaching a higher threshold keeps every
  lower badge, and completing a system does not remove the ordinary exploration tiers.
</Aside>

## Exploration

The numbered tiers count distinct worlds and need no fixed idea of how large a celestial system
ought to be. The three families use the names reported by the game, so new and modified systems can
supply their own places without being added to catlog first.

{BADGES.filter((badge) => badge.group === "exploration").map((badge) => <BadgeDetail badge={badge.badge} />)}

### Place-name families

{BADGE_FAMILIES.map((badge) => <BadgeDetail badge={badge.badge} />)}

By default, a particular place-name badge appears in the public catalogue only after two players
hold it. The award itself can exist before then; the publication threshold keeps one person's
private system names out of a community-wide list.
