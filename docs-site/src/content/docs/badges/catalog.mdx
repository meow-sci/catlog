## Exploration

The numbered tiers count distinct worlds and need no fixed idea of how large a celestial system
ought to be. The three families use the names reported by the game, so new and modified systems can
supply their own places without being added to catlog first.

Every World and Nothing Left use the complete catalogue reported by that save's game. Every World
selects its nonempty set of planets; Nothing Left includes every catalogue entry, including stars
and other bodies at the root of the system. An incomplete or interrupted catalogue awards neither.

### Place-name families

{BADGE_FAMILIES.map((badge) => <BadgeDetail badge={badge.badge} />)}

By default, a particular place-name badge appears in the public catalogue only after two players
hold it. The award itself can exist before then; the publication threshold keeps one person's
private system names out of a community-wide list. The [live JSON
catalogue](https://catlog.science.fail/v1/badges) therefore lists the fixed badges and only the
place-name members that have crossed that threshold; it is not a complete list of every award row.
